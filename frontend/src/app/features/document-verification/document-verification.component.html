Circular N° 2026-05 Reserve Requirements for Commercial Banks

3. RESERVE REQUIREMENTS

3.1 General Provisions

In accordance with Article 42 of the Banking Act, commercial banks shall maintain reserve balances in eligible accounts with the Central Bank as specified in this Circular.

Banks must maintain reserve balances in eligible accounts with the Central Bank as follows: (a) Demand deposits: 10% of the total amount; (b) Time and savings deposits: 5% of the total amount. These requirements are applicable to all banks licensed by the Central Bank.

3.2 Calculation of Reserve Requirements

Reserve requirements shall be calculated on a daily average basis using the end-of-day balances of the applicable deposit categories.

3.3 Eligible Accounts

Reserve balances must be maintained in the settlement accounts held by the bank with the Central Bank and must be freely available.
